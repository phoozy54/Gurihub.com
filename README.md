
# 🏠 GuriHub PMS - Somaliland Edition

**GuriHub** waa nidaam casri ah oo loogu talagalay maamulka hantida ma guurtada ah (Property Management System), kaas oo si gaar ah loogu habeeyay deegaanka iyo shuruucda **Somaliland**. Nidaamku wuxuu isku xiraa Mulkiilayaasha, Wakaaladaha, Kiraystayaasha, iyo Qareenada.

---

## 🚀 Tilmaamaha Muhiimka ah (Core Features)

### 🤖 GuriBot AI Assistant
*   **Falanqaynta Heshiisyada:** AI-ga ayaa baaraya heshiisyada kirada isagoo ka soo saaraya khataraha (Risks) iyo waafaqsanaanta sharciga Somaliland.
*   **Maintenance Intelligence:** AI ayaa kala saaraya ciladaha guryaha soo gaara isagoo mudnaanta siinaya (Priority Analysis).
*   **Jawaab-celin Toos ah:** GuriBot wuxuu ku jawaabayaa Af-Somali isagoo caawinaya maamulayaasha.

### 💰 Maaliyadda & Lacag-bixinta
*   **Integrated Payouts:** Taageerada tooska ah ee **Zaad** iyo **eDahab**.
*   **Warbixino Maaliyadeed:** Profit & Loss, Rent Rolls, iyo dakhliga Qurba-joogta oo si toos ah loo soo saaro.
*   **Utility Billing:** Maamulka biilasha korontada iyo biyaha ee guri walba.

### ⚖️ Portal-ka Sharciga (Lawyer Portal)
*   **Digital Notary:** Xaqiijinta heshiisyada si online ah.
*   **Contract Vetting:** Hubinta in heshiisku waafaqsan yahay Shariicada Islaamka iyo xeerarka Somaliland.

### 🌍 Qurba-Joog Connect
*   Mulkiilayaasha jooga dibadda waxay si toos ah u kormeeri karaan hantidooda iyagoo isticmaalaya Live Video Inspection iyo warbixino maaliyadeed oo live ah.

---

## 🛠 Farsamada La Adeegsaday (Tech Stack)
*   **Frontend:** React 19 + Tailwind CSS (Premium UI).
*   **AI:** Google Gemini 3 (Flash & Pro).
*   **Backend Strategy:** Lagu dhisay in lagu dul rakibo **Frappe Framework (ERPNext)**.
*   **Icons:** Lucide React.
*   **State Management:** React Context/Hooks (Modular Setup).

---

## 📂 Qaab-dhismeedka Code-ka (Project Structure)
*   `/components`: Dhammaan qaybaha UI-ga ee portals-ka.
*   `/services`: Adeegyada AI iyo isku xirka API-yada.
*   `/types.ts`: Qeexidda xogta (Data Schemas) oo u diyaarsan Frappe DocTypes.
*   `/utils`: Ogolaanshaha iyo xisaabaadka (Permissions & Helpers).

---

## 🔧 Sida loo rakibo (Future Integration)
Nidaamkan waxaa loo qaabeeyay in si fudud loogu xiro **Frappe REST API**. Waxaad u baahan doontaa:
1. Inaad abuurto DocTypes-ka u dhigma (Property, Tenant, Transaction).
2. Inaad ku xirto `apiService.ts` kaaga endpoint-yada Frappe.
